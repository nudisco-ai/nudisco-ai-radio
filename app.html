        make it your own

        on iphone or ipad, open this page in safari (not firefox or another browser).

        1. tap more (page menu), then share. if share is already visible, tap it directly.
        2. scroll down and select add to home screen. if missing, open edit actions at the bottom of the share sheet and add it.
        3. turn on open as web app, if offered, then tap add.
        4. open nudisco ai radio from your home screen.

        this app streams the station’s existing recordings. it needs a connection; it does not download the audio library.

        remember me

        this feature uses local storage to save your broadcast bookmark, schedule settings, series progress, favorites, blog post mode and pinned dress catalogue. these stay in this browser and are not sent to our servers.

        (if enabled, your saved playback bookmark, schedule settings, series progress, favorites, blog post mode and pinned dress catalogue will be deleted upon disabling this feature. clearing browser data may also remove them. what the rest of the internet does is beyond us, friend.)

        start over?

        this will return you to the beginning of the radio and replace your saved playback bookmark, if enabled.
